How we approach an investment question

  1. Understand the market and the airport

    How much traffic the airport handles, how it has moved since 2023 and how it compares with airports of similar size.

    Covered here
  2. Identify demand and capacity pressure

    Whether passengers are filling the seats on offer, what the FAA expects next and, where data is installed, whether delays point to congestion.

    Partly covered
  3. Test whether a terminal is the right intervention

    Pressure can come from runways, airspace, airline economics or the terminal itself. A lead becomes a terminal case only once the binding constraint is confirmed.

    Next diligence
  4. Communicate evidence and uncertainty

    Every answer shows its sources, periods, assumptions and the diligence still needed before capital is committed.

    Every answer

⚠A lead screen, not proof of terminal congestion, NPV or profit.
